ck-allinone — /web2/

Reserved surface for the web2 demo — the dispatch-only client front-end against CKP v3.9 Critical Isolation. Currently a stub.

This surface is mounted under busybox httpd at /web2/. Once the CK.Lib.Js v1.5.0 line lands (dispatch-only L2 facade), the next ck-allinone cut will ship the web2 demo content here directly.

In the meantime, the dispatch wire is already alive end-to-end: pgCK's -nats build owns the inbound dispatch in-extension, taking input.kernel.<K>.action.<verb> (NATS over WSS — the one data plane) and publishing the typed reply on result.kernel.<K>.<verb> with your Trace-Id echoed. Writes use the id-form input.kernel.<K>.id.<sub>.action.<verb>; the bare grammar refuses a write as unattributed. The landing page at / confirms the round-trip.

See also: /cklib/ (CK.Lib.Js bundle — what the demo will consume), /wss/ (NATS WSS endpoint routing info).
